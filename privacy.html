Who we are

ShellCheck (shell-check.com and the ShellCheck apps) is run by [your name or company, address, CVR number]. Contact: [e-mail address]. We are the data controller for the data described here.

Without an account

Your foot measurements and skate preferences are used on your device to make recommendations. They are not sent to us. We don't use advertising trackers.

With an account

  • Account: e-mail address, username, and optionally your Instagram handle. If you log in with Apple, Google, Facebook or Microsoft, we receive your name and e-mail address from them.
  • Liked skates: the skates you like, the size we suggested and your foot measurements at that time, so we can show them again and send price alerts.
  • Fit reports: how a skate fit you. Others only ever see totals (for example "8 of 10 say true to size"), never who reported what.
  • My Shell posts: photos, captions, places and skate tags you post are visible to other logged-in users, with your username and Instagram handle.
  • Notifications: if you switch on price alerts, we store a push token for your phone.

Why we use it (legal basis)

To provide the service you signed up for (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)). Price alerts and notifications only with your consent (art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw in the app at any time. Moderating reported posts is our legitimate interest in keeping the community safe (art. 6(1)(f)).

  • Supabase stores accounts and data [region you chose, e.g. EU (Frankfurt)].
  • Expo delivers push notifications.
  • [web host, e.g. Netlify] hosts this website.
  • The login providers you choose (Apple, Google, Facebook, Microsoft).

We don't sell your data.

Shop links

Links to shops may be affiliate links. When you click one, the shop and the affiliate network may set cookies to credit us for a purchase. Their own privacy policies apply on their sites.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting your account (Profile › Delete account, in the app or on the website) removes your account, likes, fit reports, posts and photos straight away.

Your rights
